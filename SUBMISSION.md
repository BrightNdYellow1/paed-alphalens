# PAED AlphaLens Submission Copy

## Product
PAED AlphaLens

## One sentence
An evidence-first quantitative research investigator that tests the assumptions behind trading signals, exposes synthetic look-ahead bias, and traces conclusions to reproducible mathematics.

## What makes it different
Rather than simply summarize a research claim, PAED AlphaLens combines bounded AI planning with deterministic Python checks, a timing counterfactual, and downloadable research evidence. Its calculations remain accessible without an AI key. A live NVIDIA Nemotron model via Nebius is required to demonstrate the full agentic workflow.

## The problem
A seemingly compelling backtest may exploit information unavailable at the time of trading. Such errors can undermine confidence in reported performance, even when an equity curve appears attractive.

## Demonstration
1. Run the deliberately future-aware synthetic case.
2. Compare reported and timing-adjusted Sharpe and equity curves.
3. Open the information timeline and source fingerprint.
4. Run the clean control and synthetic timing benchmark.
5. If live credentials are configured and a real call succeeds, run the Nemotron Investigator, showing selected approved tests and grounded interpretation.
6. Show fixed-income and portfolio interest-rate stress.
7. Export JSON evidence.

## Technology
Python, Streamlit, NumPy, pandas, pytest, OpenAI-compatible Nebius Token Factory API, NVIDIA Nemotron where live configured.

## Evaluation and limitations
The saved 40-case benchmark reports 20 correctly flagged deliberately flawed examples and 20 correctly classified clean synthetic controls. Its 100% synthetic classification result must not be presented as real-world model accuracy, LLM fidelity, independent market validation, or investment profitability. The macro scenarios are illustrative assumptions, not forecasts. The claim audit extracts candidate statements but does not verify facts.

## Submission prerequisites
- A green GitHub Actions run on the final commit.
- A live verified NVIDIA Nemotron inference through Nebius (do not claim this is complete without evidence).
- A working public Streamlit URL accessible in private browsing.
- A public video shorter than three minutes.
- Completion of the official hackathon submission form and any required licensing and attribution.
